THE STORY SO FAR

A data warehouse manager with a stable, well-paying 9-to-5 job made a bold decision. Starting in early 2025, he launched not one but three businesses simultaneously — a car wash, a kabab shop, and a crepe & waffle food truck — while continuing to show up at his desk job every single day. This is the journal of that double life. The exhaustion, the wins, the lessons, and the slow, unstoppable march toward becoming a full-time entrepreneur.
